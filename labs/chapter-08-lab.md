# Chapter 8 Lab: Prompt Crafting and Prompt Engineering for Copilot Chat

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

In Copilot Chat, craft three progressively better prompts to generate a REST API client — from vague to specific with context references and a few-shot example

## Key takeaway

Effective prompts are specific, provide context through open files or @-participants, and improve through iteration — not through sheer length.
