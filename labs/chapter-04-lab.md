# Chapter 4 Lab: Seat Management, Org Policies, and Plan Selection Scenarios

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

In a sandbox GitHub organization, navigate Settings > Copilot > Policies, toggle 'Suggestions matching public code' and assign a seat to a test user

## Key takeaway

Org policy and seat management sit above individual user control — always match compliance and licensing scenarios to the org-level settings page.
