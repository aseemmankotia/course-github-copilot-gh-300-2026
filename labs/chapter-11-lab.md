# Chapter 11 Lab: Cross-Domain Review: Scenario-Based Judgment Drills

**Estimated time:** 30 minutes

## Exercise

Complete a 20-question mixed practice set covering all seven domains under a 30-minute timer, then review each answer's domain and rationale

## Key takeaway

Cross-domain scenarios test synthesis — read every constraint carefully and choose the single option that satisfies all of them, not just one.
