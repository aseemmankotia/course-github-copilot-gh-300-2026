# Chapter 6 Lab: Privacy Fundamentals and Context Exclusions

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Create a content exclusion rule in an organization's Copilot settings to exclude a /secrets folder and verify Copilot no longer suggests completions referencing files in that path

## Key takeaway

Content exclusions are a governance control available on Business/Enterprise — configure and verify them at the correct scope before rollout.
