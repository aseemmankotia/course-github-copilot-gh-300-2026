# Chapter 10 Lab: Responsible AI and Ethical Use of Copilot

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Review a Copilot-suggested code snippet flagged as matching public code, identify the license risk, and document the decision to accept or reject per company policy

## Key takeaway

Responsible Copilot use means the human developer always reviews for bias, security, and licensing risk — accountability never transfers to the AI.
