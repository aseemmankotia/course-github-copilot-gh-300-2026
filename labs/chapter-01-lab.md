# Chapter 1 Lab: Copilot Plans and Features: Individual, Business, and Enterprise Tiers

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Walk through GitHub.com Settings > Copilot to compare plan feature toggles for a personal Individual account vs an Organization Business account

## Key takeaway

Plan selection is a judgment skill: match seat management, policy, and knowledge-base needs to the exact Copilot tier that supports them.
