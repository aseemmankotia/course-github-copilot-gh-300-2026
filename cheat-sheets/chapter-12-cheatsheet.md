# GH-300 Full Practice Exam: Pacing & Strategy Cheat Sheet

## Exam Structure
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Total questions | 65 |
| Total time | 100 minutes |
| Raw pace average | ~1.54 min/question |
| Target pace (first pass) | 1.2-1.5 min/question |
| Scaled score range | 300-1000 |
| Passing score | 700 |
| Guessing penalty | None — always answer |

⚠️ Confirm these numbers on the official GitHub certifications page before scheduling — exam providers periodically update question counts, durations, and passing scores.

## Pacing Checkpoints
| Question # | Elapsed Time Target |
|---|---|
| 15 | ~23 min |
| 30 | ~46 min |
| 45 | ~69 min |
| 65 | ~92-95 min (leaves 5-8 min buffer) |

If you're more than 5 minutes behind a checkpoint, stop reading full scenario detail on borderline questions — answer with best guess, flag, move on.

## Flag vs Skip
| Action | Answer Recorded? | Credit if time runs out? |
|---|---|---|
| Flag + guess | Yes | Yes (chance) |
| Skip / blank | No | No (guaranteed zero) |

**Rule: Never leave a question blank. Always select an answer, then flag if unsure.**

## Two-Pass Strategy
1. **First pass** — answer every question at target pace; flag anything uncertain; do not dwell past 2-3 minutes on any single question.
2. **Second pass** — use remaining buffer time exclusively on flagged questions, prioritizing ones you feel closest to solving.

## Domain-Weighted Review Priority (Study Phase)
When study time is limited before exam day, review in this order:
1. Heaviest-weighted domain first (Plans & Features)
2. Next-heaviest domains in descending weight order
3. Lightest-weighted domains last, time permitting

Rationale: maximizes expected score improvement per hour of remaining study time.

## Common Last-Mistake Traps
- **Clock panic skimming**: rushing the final 10-15 questions and misreading scenario constraints.
- **Blank answers**: leaving unanswered questions instead of guessing (guaranteed zero vs a chance).
- **Over-investing early**: spending 5+ minutes perfecting one early question and running short on later ones.
- **Ignoring flags**: flagging questions but never returning to them due to poor time tracking.

## Pre-Exam Checklist
- [ ] Verify current question count, time limit, and passing score on official page
- [ ] Confirm domain weightings haven't changed since your study materials were published
- [ ] Practice at least one full 65-question, 100-minute timed simulation
- [ ] Rehearse the flag-and-revisit workflow in a practice environment before exam day
