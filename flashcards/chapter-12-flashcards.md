## Chapter 12: GH-300 Full Practice Exam Simulation and Time-Management Strategy — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | GH-300 exam structure: question count and time limit? | 65 questions in 100 minutes. Exam tip: verify these numbers on the official GitHub certifications page before scheduling, as exam specs can be revised. |
| 2 | GH-300 average pacing target per question? | ~1.2-1.5 minutes per question, leaving buffer time for a flagged-question review pass. 100 minutes / 65 questions ≈ 1.54 min raw average. |
| 3 | GH-300 scaled scoring range and passing score? | Scaled score range: 300-1000. Passing score: 700. Exam tip: the number is not a direct percentage of raw questions correct. |
| 4 | Is there a penalty for guessing on GH-300? | No penalty for incorrect answers. Exam tip: never leave a question blank — always select a guess before time runs out. |
| 5 | Recommended pacing checkpoint interval during the exam? | Check the clock every ~15 questions to confirm you're on pace (roughly 22-23 min per 15-question block out of 100 min total). |
| 6 | Domain-weighted review priority strategy for GH-300 prep? | Review the heaviest-weighted domain (Plans & Features) first, then work down through remaining domains by weight — maximizes score gain per study hour. |
| 7 | First-pass vs second-pass exam strategy — what is it? | First pass: answer every question quickly, flagging uncertain ones. Second pass: use remaining time to revisit only flagged questions. Avoids getting stuck early. |
| 8 | Flag vs Skip — what's the key difference in exam strategy? | Flag = answer with best guess, then mark for later review (protects partial credit). Skip/leave blank = no answer recorded, guarantees zero credit if not revisited. Always flag, never leave blank. |
| 9 | What does the 'Mark for Review' / flag button do during the exam? | Bookmarks the current question in the navigation panel so you can jump directly to it later without scrolling through all 65 questions manually. |
| 10 | Before scheduling GH-300, what should you confirm on the official page? | Current question count, time limit, passing score, and domain weightings — these can be updated by the certifying body over time. |
| 11 | Recommended buffer time to leave for final review pass? | Aim to complete your first pass with 8-12 minutes remaining, reserved exclusively for revisiting flagged questions. |
| 12 | Max time to spend on a single question before flagging and moving on? | ~2-3 minutes max. If you don't have a strong direction by then, guess, flag, and move on to protect overall pacing. |
