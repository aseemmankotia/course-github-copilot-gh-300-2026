## Chapter 1: Copilot Plans and Features: Individual, Business, and Enterprise Tiers — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | Copilot Free monthly limits? | 2,000 code completions/month and 50 chat messages/month. Exam tip: Free is the only tier with hard completion caps — all paid tiers offer unlimited standard completions. |
| 2 | Copilot Pro included premium requests/month? | 300/month. Exam tip: 'Premium requests' meter access to higher-cost models; standard completions remain unlimited on Pro. |
| 3 | Copilot Pro+ included premium requests/month? | 1,500/month — 5x Pro's allowance. Exam tip: Pro+ targets power users who rely heavily on premium/frontier models. |
| 4 | Copilot Business included premium requests per user/month? | 300/user/month, same as individual Pro. Exam tip: don't confuse this with Enterprise's higher 1,000/user allowance. |
| 5 | Copilot Enterprise included premium requests per user/month? | 1,000/user/month — the highest included allowance of any tier. Exam tip: pairs with Enterprise's knowledge base and multi-org policy features. |
| 6 | Copilot Pro price? | $10/month or $100/year, billed to a personal GitHub account. Exam tip: Individual plans are self-service, never org-billed. |
| 7 | Copilot Business price? | $19 per user/month, seat-based, assigned/revoked by org admins. Exam tip: Business seats require an organization — cannot be purchased by an individual. |
| 8 | Copilot Enterprise price? | $39 per user/month, seat-based, requires GitHub Enterprise Cloud. Exam tip: same per-seat price as Copilot Pro+, but Enterprise is org-billed with admin-managed seats. |
| 9 | Which plan tier(s) support Knowledge Bases for Copilot Chat? | Copilot Enterprise only. Exam tip: Knowledge bases let Chat reference curated internal docs — this is the #1 differentiator tested between Business and Enterprise. |
| 10 | Which plan tier(s) support org-level Copilot policy controls? | Business and Enterprise (configured under Organization Settings > Copilot). Enterprise additionally supports enterprise-account policies spanning multiple orgs. Exam tip: Individual/Pro has zero policy controls. |
| 11 | Billing model: Copilot Individual vs Copilot Business/Enterprise? | Individual (Free/Pro/Pro+): billed to personal GitHub account, self-managed. Business/Enterprise: seat-based, assigned and billed centrally by org/enterprise admins. Exam tip: seat assignment/revocation is an admin action only in Business/Enterprise. |
| 12 | Which tiers include auto-generated Pull Request summaries? | Copilot Business and Enterprise. Exam tip: PR summaries are NOT available on Individual (Pro/Pro+) plans — a common feature-matrix trap. |
